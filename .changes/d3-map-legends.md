MapLegend supports grouped entries, ordered scales, details and a disclosure that can remember its state. Pass a localized `legendLabel` to MapFrame to opt into the floating legend control below 1024 px. Use `statusGlyph` and `statusGlyphSvg` from `@yesid/ui/brand` or `@yesid/ui` for the same marks shown by StatusMark.

`StatusMarkStatus` value `severe` is deprecated and still draws its diamond. Use `late` when the product classifies that status as late. Removal is eligible no earlier than v0.59.0 and 90 days after stable v0.57.0 publishes.
